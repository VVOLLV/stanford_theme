A purposeful university

Stanford was founded almost 150 years ago on a bedrock of societal purpose. Our mission is to contribute to the world by educating students for lives of leadership and contribution with integrity; advancing fundamental knowledge and cultivating creativity; and accelerating solutions that improve the human condition.

Science & Technology

Stanford researchers develop new AI model for predicting protein structures

A team from the School of Engineering and the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI) has unveiled a novel deep-learning architecture that predicts three-dimensional protein folding with 40% greater accuracy than current state-of-the-art methods. The breakthrough promises to accelerate drug discovery, deepen our understanding of biological systems, and open new pathways for treating neurodegenerative diseases, including Alzheimer's and Parkinson's.

Research & Impact

Discovery

Advancing knowledge, accelerating solutions for humanity

Stanford's culture of collaboration drives innovative discoveries that address the world's most urgent challenges. From pioneering CRISPR gene-editing therapies and building the world's most powerful X-ray laser at SLAC, to reimagining the future of artificial intelligence through HAI and leading global climate policy through the Woods Institute — Stanford researchers are shaping the future across every frontier of knowledge.

Admission

Stanford offers unparalleled opportunities to learn, discover, and grow. Whether you're an undergraduate exploring your passions through our flexible curriculum, a graduate student pushing the boundaries of your field, or a lifelong learner expanding your horizons — you'll find a vibrant community of scholars dedicated to making a meaningful difference in the world.

With need-blind admission for U.S. undergraduates and generous financial aid ensuring that family income is never a barrier to a Stanford education, we invite you to bring your curiosity, ambition, and unique perspective to the Farm.
